# EduSaaS — Assessment Guidance

## Overview

EduSaaS assessments are designed to help learners understand their current knowledge level, identify learning gaps, and determine suitable educational pathways.

Assessments may be used before enrollment, during a course, or as part of academic progress evaluation.

---

## Purpose of Assessments

Assessments can help:

- Evaluate a learner's current knowledge
- Identify strengths and weaknesses
- Understand prerequisite readiness
- Recommend an appropriate learning path
- Measure progress during a program
- Identify topics that require additional practice

---

## Assessment Types

Depending on the program, EduSaaS may provide different types of assessments:

### Knowledge Assessment

Tests understanding of fundamental concepts related to the selected subject.

### Technical Assessment

Evaluates technical knowledge and problem-solving ability.

### Skill Assessment

Measures practical skills relevant to a particular learning program.

### Progress Assessment

Evaluates a learner's understanding after completing parts of a course or program.

---

## Assessment Process

A typical assessment process can include:

1. Learner selects or is assigned an assessment.
2. Learner receives assessment instructions.
3. Learner completes the assessment.
4. Responses are submitted for evaluation.
5. Results are generated.
6. The learner receives feedback or guidance where applicable.

---

## Assessment Eligibility

Assessment eligibility depends on the specific assessment or program.

Some assessments may be available to prospective learners, while others may require enrollment in a particular program.

The AI agent must not assume eligibility requirements unless they are documented for the specific assessment.

---

## Assessment Results

Assessment results may provide information such as:

- Overall performance
- Topic-wise performance
- Strength areas
- Areas requiring improvement
- Recommended learning areas

Results should be interpreted according to the assessment's documented evaluation criteria.

---

## Assessment Preparation

Learners may prepare by:

- Reviewing prerequisite concepts
- Studying relevant course material
- Practicing subject-specific problems
- Reviewing previous learning material
- Understanding the assessment instructions before starting

---

## Assessment Support

If a learner has difficulty understanding the assessment process, EduSaaS counselors or support personnel can provide guidance according to the applicable support process.

The AI agent should explain only the support options documented in the knowledge base.

---

## Common Questions

### Why does EduSaaS conduct assessments?

Assessments help understand a learner's current knowledge and identify suitable learning or improvement areas.

### Do I need an assessment before enrolling?

This depends on the specific program or assessment requirements.

### What happens after completing an assessment?

The learner's responses are evaluated and results or feedback may be provided according to the applicable assessment process.

### Can I retake an assessment?

Retake availability depends on the specific assessment policy. The AI agent must not promise a retake unless the applicable policy confirms it.

### Can the assessment determine which course I should take?

An assessment may help identify suitable learning areas, but course recommendations should be based on the learner's requirements and the relevant EduSaaS course information.

### How can I get help with an assessment?

The learner should use the documented EduSaaS support or counseling channel for assistance.

---

## AI Agent Rules

- Do not invent assessment scores or results.
- Do not guarantee admission based on assessment performance.
- Do not guarantee course eligibility unless documented.
- Do not promise retakes unless the applicable policy allows them.
- Do not invent assessment duration, number of questions, passing scores, or evaluation criteria.
- Use course-specific documents when the learner asks about a particular course.
- Use enrollment documents for enrollment-related questions.
- Use policy documents when the question concerns assessment rules.
- If required information is unavailable, clearly state that it is not available in the current knowledge base.

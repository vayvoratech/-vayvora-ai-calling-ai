EduSaaS Overview
EduSaaS is the technology education and engineering upskilling division affiliated with Vayvora Technology. It delivers intensive online training programs across modern software disciplines with automated coding assessments, AI voice mock interviews, and production capstone projects.
Common Program Information
- Tuition fee: ₹5,000 INR per program track.
- Student Track: Structured for university schedules with placement preparation, portfolio development, and resume building.
- Working Professional Track: Evening and weekend cohorts focused on scalable system architectures and senior-level upskilling.
- Official enrollment portal: edusaas.vayvora.com
- Official email: info@edusaas.vayvora.com

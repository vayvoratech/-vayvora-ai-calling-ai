## Enrollment Overview

The EduSaaS enrollment process consists of three primary stages:

1. Application Submission
2. Admissions Consultation
3. Registration Confirmation

The process is designed to collect basic learner information, understand the learner's educational goals, and confirm registration.

---

## Step 1 — Application Submission

The learner begins the enrollment process by providing basic information about their background and learning objectives.

### Information Collected

The learner may provide:

- Full name
- Educational background
- Current education status
- Previous technical experience
- Area of interest
- Target learning track
- Preferred learning format
- Contact information

### Purpose

The application information helps EduSaaS understand the learner's background and identify the appropriate learning track.

---

## Step 2 — Admissions Consultation

After submitting an application, the learner may participate in an admissions consultation with an academic counselor.

### Consultation Format

The consultation may be conducted through:

- Phone
- Video call

### Consultation Duration

The sample enrollment process specifies a **20-minute consultation**.

### Consultation Topics

The academic counselor may discuss:

- Learner's educational background
- Learning objectives
- Technical experience
- Target specialization
- Course expectations
- Learning format
- Program suitability
- Questions from the learner

The counselor may use this discussion to provide guidance about the learner's selected learning track.

---

## Step 3 — Registration Confirmation

After the admissions process is completed, the learner receives registration confirmation.

### Confirmation Method

The sample process specifies that the learner receives a welcome syllabus packet through a **verified email address**.

### Confirmation Information

The confirmation communication may contain:

- Registration confirmation
- Welcome information
- Syllabus information
- Program-related instructions
- Next steps

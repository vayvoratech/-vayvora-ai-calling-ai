# EduSaaS — Admissions Policy

> [!NOTE]
> This document contains sample admissions information for RAG testing. Replace sample details with officially verified EduSaaS policies before production use.

## Admissions Overview

EduSaaS admissions are intended to determine whether a prospective learner meets the basic educational or experience requirements for a learning program.

The admissions process may also be used to understand the learner's background, learning objectives, and available enrollment options.

---

## Eligibility

### Educational Requirement

Applicants must have completed secondary education or an equivalent level of education.

### Professional Experience

Equivalent professional experience may be considered where applicable.

Applicants who do not follow the standard educational pathway may discuss their professional background during the admissions process.

### Eligibility Evaluation

Eligibility may be reviewed during the admissions process.

The AI agent must not guarantee eligibility unless the applicable admissions process confirms it.

---

## Admissions Interview

The admissions interview may be used to understand:

- Educational background
- Professional experience
- Learning objectives
- Target learning track
- Previous technical experience
- Program requirements
- Enrollment preferences

The applicant may use the interview to ask questions about the applicable program and enrollment process.

---

## Financial Arrangements

### Installment Arrangements

Installment arrangements may be available depending on the applicable admission circumstances and cohort availability.

The specific arrangement is determined during the admissions interview.

The AI agent must not promise a particular installment plan unless it is explicitly confirmed by the applicable admissions process.

### Financial Assistance

Partial financial assistance may be available in some circumstances.

Eligibility and availability are determined during the admissions interview and depend on cohort availability.

The AI agent must not guarantee financial assistance.

---

## Cohort Availability

Admissions and financial arrangements may depend on available cohort capacity.

Availability should be confirmed through the appropriate EduSaaS admissions process.

The AI agent must not claim that a specific cohort has available seats unless current availability is provided by an authorized system or knowledge-base document.

---

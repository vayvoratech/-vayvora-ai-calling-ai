# JB's Serene City

## Overview

JB's Serene City is a residential development by JB Infra located in Ibrahimpatnam, Hyderabad.

The project includes plotted development along with villas and commercial spaces.

## Location

Ibrahimpatnam, Hyderabad, Telangana.

## Approval

The official JB Infra website describes Serene City as HMDA approved.

## Project Features

The project includes:

- Plotted development
- Villas
- Commercial spaces
- 80 ft black-top roads
- Solar fencing
- Grand clubhouse
- Resort-style amenities

## Positioning

Serene City is positioned as a community suitable for:

- Families
- Property investors
- People looking to build homes
- Customers interested in plotted developments

# JB's Nature Valley

## Overview

JB's Nature Valley is an integrated township project by JB Infra.

The project covers approximately 700 acres.

## Project Highlights

According to the official JB Infra website:

- 700-acre integrated township
- 6,000+ residential plots
- 100 ft wide roads
- 9 km cycling tracks
- 30% dedicated green zones
- Multiple development phases

## Concept

Nature Valley is designed around the idea of combining residential development with greenery, open spaces, and modern infrastructure.

The project is positioned as an eco-friendly satellite township.

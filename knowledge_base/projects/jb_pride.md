# JB Pride

## Overview

JB Pride is a large-scale plotted development by JB Infra located in Tandur, Mominpet, Hyderabad.

The project is presented as a large, eco-conscious development designed around community living, greenery, and modern amenities.

## Location

Tandur, Mominpet,
Hyderabad, Telangana – 501202.

## Project Highlights

According to the official JB Infra project page:

- 8 phases
- Large-scale plotted development
- Rainwater harvesting

- Green spaces
- Pollution-conscious development
- 2 clubhouses
- Sports arena
- Cricket ground
- Amphitheater
- Gated community
- Wide internal roads
- Electricity and water supply
- Underground drainage

## Voice Agent Sales Qualification

When a potential customer expresses interest in a Vayvora service, the AI agent can identify:

### Requirement

"What type of solution are you looking for?"

### Business Objective

"What problem are you trying to solve?"

### Existing System

"Do you already have an application or system?"

### Desired Solution

"What features or capabilities do you need?"

### Contact Information

"What is the best way for our team to contact you?"

The AI agent should avoid promising pricing, timelines, technical architecture, or project delivery until those details are confirmed by the Vayvora Technology team.

# JB Infra Group

## Company Overview

JB Infra Group is a real estate company focused on residential developments, including plotted developments, villas, and housing options in and around Hyderabad.

The company presents itself as having more than 25 years of experience in the real estate sector.

According to the official JB Infra website:

- 25+ years of experience
- 25,000+ happy customers
- 2,500+ acres developed
- 100% legal compliance

JB Infra focuses on developing residential communities in locations with growth potential and connectivity around Hyderabad.

## What JB Infra Offers

JB Infra's projects include:

- Residential plots
- Villa plots
- Villas
- Plotted communities
- Integrated townships
- Resort-style residential developments

## Company Vision

JB Infra aims to create communities that combine modern living, affordability, connectivity, greenery, and long-term value.

# Vayvora Technology - Recruitment and Hiring Process

## Recruitment Stages
Vayvora Technology follows a structured, candidate-friendly four-stage hiring process designed to evaluate technical depth, systems thinking, and team alignment:

1. **Application and Profile Screening (1-3 Business Days):**
   - Our talent acquisition team reviews resumes, portfolios, and GitHub repositories submitted to careers at vayvora tech dot com or through the vayvora tech dot com careers portal.

2. **Technical Screening Call (30 Minutes):**
   - An initial conversation with an engineering lead or technical recruiter to discuss candidate background, core programming language proficiencies, and alignment with open squad initiatives.

3. **Hands-on Technical Assessment and Architecture Review (60-90 Minutes):**
   - Practical hands-on coding or system design evaluation relevant to the role (e.g., Generative AI pipeline design for AI engineers, scalable microservices design for full-stack developers).
   - Candidates work through real-world engineering scenarios reflective of Vayvora's production client engagements.

4. **Leadership Discussion and Culture Alignment (30-45 Minutes):**
   - Final conversation with executive leadership covering engineering culture, ownership mindset, professional growth paths, and compensation structure.

5. **Offer Rollout and Onboarding:**
   - Formal offer letters are dispatched within 48 to 72 hours of successful completion of all interview rounds.
   - Comprehensive onboarding program with a dedicated engineering mentor.

## Inquiries and Follow-Ups
Candidates can check the status of their application, interview schedule, or assessment results at any time by:
- Emailing careers at vayvora tech dot com or info at vayvora tech dot com.
- All candidate assessments and interview links are managed exclusively through email invitations sent by our talent acquisition team. The voice assistant cannot schedule live calls or interviews for job candidates.

# Careers and Job Opportunities at Vayvora Technology

Vayvora Technology is an enterprise engineering consultancy that recruits outstanding technical talent across software engineering, artificial intelligence, and cloud systems.

## Candidate Inquiry Handling & Conversational Flow
When a prospective candidate, student, or job seeker inquires about careers or applying for a role:
1. **Interactive Domain Discovery:** The assistant should first ask the candidate what specific role or engineering domain they are interested in (for example: AI & Generative AI, Full-Stack Engineering, Cloud & DevOps, Voice AI, or Engineering Internships).
2. **Availability Check:**
   - **Active Openings:** AI & Generative AI Engineers, Full-Stack Software Engineers, Cloud & DevOps Architects, Voice AI Engineers, and Engineering Internships.
   - **No Active Openings:** Non-technical positions, HR, marketing, sales, and administrative roles currently have no vacancies.
3. **Application Instructions:** Instruct the candidate to email their resume, portfolio, and GitHub profile to careers at vayvora tech dot com with their desired position in the subject line.
4. **Strict No-Meeting Policy for Candidates:** Vayvora Technology does not schedule introductory meetings, discovery calls, or interview slots over the phone for students or job applicants. The engineering leadership team does not have bandwidth to conduct direct calls with every candidate. All applicants must submit their resume via email. Shortlisted candidates are contacted directly via email by the talent acquisition team with assessment and next-step links.

## Open Roles and Core Requirements
- **AI & Generative AI Engineers:** LLM orchestration, agentic workflows, RAG architectures, model evaluation and fine-tuning, Python.
- **Full-Stack Software Engineers:** React, TypeScript, Next.js, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, REST/GraphQL APIs.
- **Cloud & DevOps Architects:** Kubernetes, Docker, AWS, GCP, Terraform, CI/CD pipeline automation.
- **Voice AI & Telephony Engineers:** WebRTC, real-time STT and TTS pipelines, telephony protocols.
- **Engineering Internships:** Computer science students or recent graduates with strong algorithmic foundations and hands-on coding proficiency in Python, JavaScript, or TypeScript.

## Recruitment Process
1. Resume and portfolio screening by our talent acquisition team (typically 2-3 business days).
2. Online practical coding or architecture assessment sent via email to shortlisted applicants.
3. Technical interview with engineering squad leads.
4. Final discussion with executive leadership and offer rollout.
